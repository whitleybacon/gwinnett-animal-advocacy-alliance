Become a GAAA Volunteer Advocate

GAAA is powered by community members who volunteer their time, skills, and experience to support animal welfare advocacy in Gwinnett County.

Volunteer Advocates may help with community outreach, volunteer coordination, email and marketing, public records and research, social media outreach, government affairs, policy work, and other advocacy efforts.

Volunteer Advocate Interest Form

Interested in becoming more actively involved with GAAA? Complete the form below to tell us a little about yourself, your interests, skills, and availability.

After you submit the form, a member of our team will reach out to schedule a brief conversation and discuss opportunities that may be a good fit. Volunteer Advocates complete our onboarding process and Volunteer Advocate Agreement & Code of Conduct before joining an internal GAAA committee or volunteer group.

Please note: GAAA Volunteer Advocates are volunteers supporting our advocacy work and are not employees, official representatives, or spokespersons for GAAA unless specifically authorized. This is also separate from volunteering directly with Gwinnett County Animal Welfare & Enforcement.
