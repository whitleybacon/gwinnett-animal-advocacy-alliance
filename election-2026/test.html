Animal Welfare & the 2026 Election

Learn how candidates seeking to represent Gwinnett County in county and state government are engaging with animal-welfare issues affecting our community.

Your 2026 Animal Welfare Election Guide

GAAA's 2026 Election Project provides Gwinnett residents with information about candidate participation, responses and public commitments on animal-welfare issues at both the county and state level.

Our goal is simple: give voters clear, accessible information before they cast their ballots.

After the Election

A Public Record of What Candidates Said

This project does not end on Election Day. Candidate responses, signed pledges, outreach records and participation information will remain publicly available so residents can revisit what candidates said and compare those positions with future votes, actions and policy decisions by those who take office.

A candidate's response to GAAA, meeting with GAAA, attendance at a GAAA event, participation in a Candidate Survey, or signature on an Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge does not constitute an endorsement by Gwinnett Animal Advocacy Alliance. Candidate responses will be presented without changing the substance of their answers.
