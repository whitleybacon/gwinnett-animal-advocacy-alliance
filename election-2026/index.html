2026 Election Guide

Your 2026 Animal Welfare Election Guide

The 2026 election will determine who represents Districts 2 and 4 on the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners.

GAAA created this Election Hub to bring our election-related resources together in one place. Here, Gwinnett voters can learn about the candidates, review the questions candidates were asked, follow Candidate Pledge participation, and — as responses are received — compare where candidates stand on issues affecting animals and residents across our county.

Our goal is simple: give voters clear, accessible information before they cast their ballots.

After the Election

A Public Record of What Candidates Said

The purpose of this project is not only to help residents make informed decisions before Election Day. Candidate responses and signed pledges can also create a public record that residents can revisit after the election to evaluate whether stated positions and commitments translate into action.

Participation in GAAA's Candidate Survey or signing the Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge does not constitute an endorsement by Gwinnett Animal Advocacy Alliance. Candidate responses will be presented without changing the substance of their answers.
