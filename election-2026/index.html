Your 2026 Animal Welfare Election Guide

The 2026 election will determine who represents Districts 2 and 4 on the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners. Explore candidate participation, survey responses and pledge commitments to see where candidates stand on animal-welfare issues before casting your ballot.

Our goal is simple: give voters clear, accessible information before they cast their ballots.

After the Election

A Public Record of What Candidates Said

This project does not end on Election Day. Candidate responses, signed pledges and participation records will remain publicly available so residents can revisit what candidates said and compare those positions with future votes, actions and policy decisions by those who take office.

Participation in GAAA's Candidate Survey or signing the Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge does not constitute an endorsement by Gwinnett Animal Advocacy Alliance. Candidate responses will be presented without changing the substance of their answers.
