2026 Gwinnett County Election

Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge

A commitment to transparent leadership, independent oversight, humane care and measurable action for Gwinnett County's animals and residents.

More Than a Campaign Promise

Supporting animal welfare is easy to say. Meaningful change requires action.

The GAAA 2026 Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge asks candidates for the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners to make clear, public commitments on the policies, oversight, funding and leadership decisions that directly affect animals throughout our community.

By signing the GAAA Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge, I commit to using my position on the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners to advance humane, transparent and accountable animal-welfare policies and to ensure that animals entrusted to Gwinnett County receive the care and protection they deserve.

1

Independent Oversight & Community Voice

I pledge to support meaningful independent oversight and community representation in Gwinnett's animal-welfare system.

I will support an Animal Advisory Council that can independently ask questions, review information, request records and data, make recommendations and advise the Board of Commissioners without interference or retaliation.

I will support meaningful citizen and animal-advocate representation and a Council structure that maintains independence from the department it is charged with advising.

2

Accountability, Transparency & Protection for Those Who Speak Up

I pledge to hold Gwinnett Animal Welfare leadership and employees to meaningful standards of professionalism, animal care, regulatory compliance and accountability.

When credible concerns involving animal treatment, employee conduct, shelter operations or violations of law or policy are raised, I will seek answers, ensure concerns are appropriately investigated and follow up on corrective action rather than simply defer to the department.

I will support protections for employees, volunteers, Council members and others who raise legitimate concerns in good faith without fear of retaliation.

3

Independent Review & Objective Oversight

I pledge to support a comprehensive independent operational audit and/or independent task force to evaluate Gwinnett's animal-welfare system, including animal care, shelter operations, workplace culture, policies, programs and outcomes.

Any resulting recommendations should include measurable goals, implementation timelines and public progress reporting.

I also support appropriate camera coverage in animal housing, medical and animal-handling areas to protect animals and employees and provide objective evidence when incidents or complaints occur.

4

Stronger Animal Protection Laws & Enforcement

I pledge to support effective local ordinances that protect animals and address preventable causes of shelter intake, including:

  • stronger measures addressing irresponsible and backyard breeding
  • prohibiting the retail sale of dogs, cats and rabbits from commercial breeding sources
  • strengthening ordinances when existing laws are inadequate to protect animals
  • consistent enforcement of existing animal-welfare laws and ordinances
  • adequate resources and staffing for Animal Enforcement to effectively enforce those laws

I will support responsible pet-ownership initiatives while ensuring enforcement is fair, consistent and focused on improving animal and community welfare.

5

Prevention, Humane Care, Open Intake & Lifesaving

I pledge to prioritize policies and programs that prevent animals from entering the shelter whenever safely possible and improve the quality of life of animals who do enter County care.

This includes supporting:

  • accessible and affordable spay/neuter programs
  • expanded TNR and community-cat programs
  • owner-retention and surrender-prevention resources
  • stronger foster, rescue, adoption and reunification partnerships
  • meaningful daily enrichment, exercise and opportunities outside the kennel
  • individualized intervention for long-stay and at-risk animals
  • modernization of the volunteer program and removal of unnecessary barriers to meaningful participation
  • adequate and timely veterinary care
  • stronger oversight and measurable performance standards for contracted veterinary providers
  • development of permanent in-house veterinary capacity, including County-employed veterinarians and veterinary technicians
  • meaningful euthanasia safeguards, advance notice and opportunities for rescue, foster or adoption placement whenever circumstances allow
Open-intake commitment: I will support maintaining Gwinnett County Animal Welfare & Enforcement as an open-intake public shelter that accepts stray, abandoned and owner-surrendered animals needing County shelter services, while investing in prevention, foster and rescue partnerships, emergency overflow capacity, and additional shelter infrastructure when necessary to ensure animals are safely cared for rather than turned away or unnecessarily euthanized because of insufficient space.

I will support strategies designed to reduce unnecessary euthanasia and prevent animals from deteriorating physically or behaviorally because of prolonged shelter confinement.

6

Public Data, Responsiveness & Budget Accountability

I pledge to support transparent public reporting of meaningful animal-welfare data so residents and elected officials can evaluate how Gwinnett's animal-welfare system is performing without relying on repeated Open Records Requests.

I also pledge to meaningfully review and respond to documented concerns, data and recommendations submitted by residents and community organizations. When credible information identifies potential problems or opportunities for improvement, it should receive acknowledgment, evaluation and a substantive response — not simply be ignored.

Public reporting should include meaningful information regarding shelter intake and outcomes, euthanasia and contributing circumstances, length of stay, shelter capacity, medical care, enrichment, volunteer participation, prevention programs and other key performance measures.

I will support making animal welfare a meaningful County budget priority and advocate for appropriate resources for veterinary care, prevention, enforcement, enrichment, staffing, volunteer/foster programs, technology, shelter capacity and other programs demonstrated to improve outcomes.

Programs receiving taxpayer funding should have measurable objectives, publicly reported results and meaningful oversight when those results indicate improvement is needed.

7

Leadership & A Commitment to Act

I recognize that animal welfare is a responsibility of County government and that Commissioners have a responsibility to provide oversight, establish policy and allocate resources.

I pledge to listen to residents, animal advocates, volunteers, employees, professionals and advisory bodies; examine evidence and data; ask difficult questions; and follow up when concerns are raised.

When evidence demonstrates that a policy, program or practice is failing animals or residents, I will advocate for change — even when doing so means challenging the status quo.

I will not consider support for animal welfare fulfilled simply by expressing concern. I commit to using the authority of the office to pursue meaningful, measurable improvements.

For Candidates

The Candidate Pledge

Candidates receiving the 2026 GAAA Candidate Survey will also receive a copy of the Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge. Candidates may sign and return the pledge to publicly affirm their commitment to the principles outlined above.

Completing the Candidate Survey and signing the Candidate Pledge are separate opportunities for candidates to communicate their positions and commitments to Gwinnett County voters.
