Question 3

What is the primary reason you are running for the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners?

Benjamin Culberson’s Response

Question 5

Prior to receiving this questionnaire, how familiar were you with current animal-welfare issues in Gwinnett County?

Benjamin Culberson’s Response
Slightly familiar
Question 6

Based on your current knowledge, what do you believe are the most significant animal-welfare challenges facing Gwinnett County, and why?

Benjamin Culberson’s Response

Volunteer recruitment: the time and effort committed by volunteers should be respected with swift intake processes, fair and adequate training, and proper representation.

Animal intake: we need to ensure Gwinnett County animal care centers are properly supplied to ensure public safety goals are reached and the best interest of the animals is kept in mind.

Support
