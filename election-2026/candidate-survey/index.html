Candidate Survey Underway

Responses Due August 21, 2026

GAAA is inviting candidates for Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners Districts 2 and 4 to complete our 2026 Animal Welfare Candidate Survey. Candidate responses will be published here after the response deadline.

Animal welfare decisions are made at the ballot box, too.

Decisions made by the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners directly affect shelter operations, animal care, public safety and enforcement, funding, prevention programs, oversight, infrastructure and long-term animal-welfare priorities.

GAAA created this survey to give voters more than campaign talking points. Candidates are being asked where they stand on specific policies and what actions they would support if elected.

What We're Asking Candidates

The 68-question survey addresses the major policy, funding and oversight decisions that shape animal welfare in Gwinnett County.

Oversight & Accountability

  • Animal Advisory Council independence
  • Citizen representation
  • Transparency and public records
  • Independent review and cameras

Humane Care & Lifesaving

  • Enrichment and behavioral welfare
  • Veterinary and in-house medical care
  • Euthanasia safeguards
  • Rescue, foster and adoption opportunities

Prevention & Protection

  • Spay/neuter and TNVR
  • Breeding and retail pet sales
  • Owner retention and reunification
  • Animal-protection ordinances and enforcement

Capacity, Community & Resources

  • Open intake and shelter capacity
  • Emergency overflow planning
  • Volunteer and rescue partnerships
  • Budget priorities and performance measures

Built Around Gwinnett's Actual Animal-Welfare Issues

This is not a generic candidate questionnaire. GAAA developed the 2026 survey from the issues we have researched, documented and advocated on in Gwinnett County.

Records & Data

County records, shelter data, medical records, contracts, investigations and operational information help establish the factual basis for many of the questions.

Independent Analysis

GAAA's audits and issue analyses examine areas such as enrichment, managed intake, veterinary care, volunteer engagement, community cats and oversight.

Community Experience

Feedback from residents, volunteers, advocates, rescue partners and others helps identify where policies and practices affect animals and the people trying to help them.
