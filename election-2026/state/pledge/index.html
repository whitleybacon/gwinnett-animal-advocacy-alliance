Georgia General Assembly

2026 Animal Welfare Candidate Pledge

Gwinnett Animal Advocacy Alliance developed this pledge to give candidates for the Georgia General Assembly an opportunity to publicly demonstrate their commitment to animal-welfare issues affecting communities throughout Georgia.

As a candidate for the Georgia General Assembly, I pledge to support practical, effective efforts that improve the humane treatment and protection of animals throughout Georgia. If elected, I commit to:
1

Prevent pet overpopulation and unnecessary shelter intake.

Support sustainable funding and policies that expand access to affordable spay/neuter services and other effective prevention programs throughout Georgia, while addressing preventable causes of shelter intake and pet surrender, including barriers that make it difficult for families to keep their pets.

2

Protect local animal-welfare ordinances from state preemption bills.

Oppose legislation that unnecessarily removes or restricts the authority of local governments to adopt and enforce animal-welfare ordinances that address the needs of their communities.

3

Strengthen Georgia's animal-cruelty laws when needed.

Support thoughtful legislation that protects animals from cruelty, aggravated cruelty, severe neglect, abandonment, animal fighting, and other serious mistreatment, including efforts to identify and address gaps or inconsistencies in existing law.

4

Support effective enforcement of animal-protection laws.

Support appropriate tools, training, coordination, and resources for the agencies, law-enforcement personnel, prosecutors, and other officials responsible for investigating animal cruelty and enforcing Georgia's animal-protection laws.

5

Protect animals from repeat offenders.

Support effective mechanisms for identifying and addressing repeat animal-cruelty offenders and for ensuring that protections for animals remain meaningful after serious or repeated violations.

6

Address irresponsible breeding and the commercial sale of companion animals.

Support reasonable, enforceable policies that promote responsible breeding and appropriate standards of care for animals in commercial breeding facilities, including those commonly referred to as “puppy mills,” and address breeding and commercial-sale practices that contribute to animal cruelty, neglect, abandonment, consumer harm, and shelter overpopulation.

7

Support strong standards of care and oversight for animals in regulated facilities.

Support effective, enforceable standards that protect the health, safety, and humane treatment of animals in shelters, rescues, kennels, breeding operations, pet dealers and other facilities regulated by the State, along with appropriate oversight and enforcement of those standards.

8

Support transparency and useful statewide animal-welfare data.

Support efforts to improve the accessibility, accuracy, and usefulness of statewide animal shelter data so policymakers and the public can understand shelter intake and outcomes, enforcement trends, and other animal-welfare issues and evaluate whether policies are working.

9

Include animal-welfare stakeholders in the legislative process.

Listen to animal-welfare advocates, veterinarians, shelter professionals, rescues, law enforcement, prosecutors, subject-matter experts, and Georgia residents when considering legislation or policy affecting animals and Georgia's animal-welfare systems.

10

Work across party lines on animal welfare.

Seek practical bipartisan solutions that protect animals, prevent cruelty, reduce unnecessary shelter intake, and improve animal welfare throughout Georgia.

11

Be responsive when constituents raise animal-welfare concerns.

When constituents bring forward significant animal-welfare concerns, communicate with appropriate local and state officials—including county commissioners and animal-welfare agencies—to better understand the issue, help identify available solutions, and determine whether state action or assistance is appropriate.

By signing this pledge, I affirm my commitment to these principles and to continued dialogue with Georgia's animal-welfare community.
