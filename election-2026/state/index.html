State Legislative Outreach

Bringing Animal Welfare Into the State-Level Conversation

GAAA identified candidates seeking Georgia House and Senate seats representing Gwinnett County and began direct outreach to introduce our organization, discuss animal-welfare issues, and establish communication with candidates across party lines.

This page documents that outreach and candidate participation. Candidate status will be updated as additional responses and conversations occur.

This is a separate project from GAAA's Gwinnett County Commission Candidate Survey and Candidate Pledge. The County project focuses on policies and oversight within the authority of the Gwinnett County Board of Commissioners. This project focuses on candidates for the Georgia General Assembly and animal-welfare issues that may involve state law or state-level policy.
